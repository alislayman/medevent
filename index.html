Product specification

Medical Event Staffing Platform

A two-sided marketplace connecting medical professionals — doctors, nurses, officials, cutmen, ambulance staff — with event organisers who need qualified on-site medical coverage.

Working titleDraft · 11 Jul 2026Inspiration · MED IN GULF · Malt · Futboljobs

01 — Overview

Overview

The platform connects medical professionals with event organisers who need medical staff on site — sports competitions, combat-sports events and public gatherings.

Two user types

  • Professionals — build a verified profile, set specialties, trainings, availability and experience, then search and apply for events. They can also form teams with their peers and apply together.
  • Organisations — post events with requirements, attach a contract, and search and invite professionals. They can see which team a professional belongs to and its members.

Core loop

  • An organiser creates an event with requirements; the event is either public or sent privately to support.
  • Professionals discover events and apply; organisers discover professionals and invite them.
  • Professionals can form teams, invite peers and apply together as a team; organisers can view a professional's team and its members.
  • The two parties communicate, agree on a contract, and the engagement proceeds.

How it makes money

  • Monthly subscription tiers for professionals — Basic, Bronze, Silver, Gold.
  • Free signup for organisations, with payable premium services.
  • A paid verification badge for professionals.

03 — Technical stack

Technical stack

The backend language is fixed (Python). The rest is chosen for a robust, GDPR-appropriate build that also serves a future mobile app from one codebase.

  • Backend

    Django + Django REST Framework

    One clean, versioned API serving both the web app and the future mobile app, with a dedicated back-office for verification, support and quotations.

  • Frontend · web

    Next.js (React) + TypeScript

    Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui for a responsive, accessible interface; server-side rendering for fast, SEO-friendly public pages.

  • Mobile

    Responsive → PWA → native

    Responsive web first, then an installable PWA with push, then a native iOS + Android app via React Native / Expo.

  • Database

    PostgreSQL

    Relational core with JSONB for flexible fields and full-text search to power matching and filtering.

  • Auth & payments

    JWT · Stripe · PayPal

    Django auth with JWT for the API; Stripe (cards + tiers) and PayPal, with webhooks keeping subscription state in sync.

  • File storage

    EU S3-compatible storage

    Encrypted object storage in the EU for avatars and sensitive credential documents.

  • AI

    AI search & support chatbot

    AI-assisted matching and ranking in search, plus a support chatbot layered on the same API in a later phase.

  • Hosting

    EU / France · GDPR

    EU data residency (Scaleway, OVHcloud or Hetzner) for medical and credential data.

  • Source & CI/CD

    GitHub + GitHub Actions

    Automated lint → test → build → deploy; mobile builds via Expo EAS.

06 — Public pages

Public pages (no sign-in)

Home page

  • Intro video, logo, clean and minimal interface, value proposition.
  • Clear calls to action: sign up as a Professional or as an Organisation.

Other public pages

  • Licensing page.
  • About Us page.
  • Recommended additions: Pricing / Plans, Contact, and legal pages (GDPR privacy policy, terms of service).

07 — Professional profile

Professional profile

  • Professional type: Médecin, Infirmier, Officiel, Cutman, Ambulancier.
  • Medical specialty: one or several.
  • Diploma: year, place, university, country.

Complementary trainings (treated like diplomas)

  • Systematic (predefined) options:
    • Traumatologie du sport
    • Gestion d’urgence terrain
    • ATLS
    • PHTLS
  • Free-text field for other trainings.
  • Document upload (included in the verification).

Registration with the medical board (tableau de l’ordre)

  • Département / region
  • Date
  • Document
  • Country(ies) where the license is valid

Experience & other fields

  • Event volume / experience captured as ranges (0–5, 5–10, …).
  • Languages.
  • Certificates → document uploads.
  • Availability (recommended): calendar or date ranges so organisers can filter by who is free.

08 — Organisation profile

Organisation profile

  • Organisation details: name, type, country, logo.
  • Requirements: specialties / roles typically needed, event types, typical locations.
  • Contact and billing information.
These fields are indicative and should be expanded and explained in more detail — the full field list, formats and which are mandatory need to be defined.

09 — Events & contracts

Events & contracts

Event creation (by organiser)

  • Details: title, date(s), location, roles / specialties required, number of professionals, budget or range.

Contract

  • A contract is auto-generated by the system and attached to the event.
  • The organiser can edit or replace specific lines, or attach their own contract instead.

Visibility

  • Public — listed and searchable by professionals.
  • Direct to support — sent privately to support for handling.

Application / invitation

  • Professionals apply to public events individually or as a team.
  • Organisers invite professionals and can see which team a professional belongs to and its members (§10).

10 — Teams

Teams

Teams are a professional feature — they are not owned by organisations. Professionals group together, invite each other and can apply to events as a single team.

Professionals

  • Create a team and become its owner; invite peers to join.
  • A professional can belong to several teams at once (multi-team membership).
  • Apply to public events as a team, in addition to applying individually.

Organisations

  • Can see which team a professional belongs to and view the team's members.
  • Can invite a professional and, through them, reach the team — but do not create or manage teams.

11 — Search & matching

Search & matching

  • Professional searches for events (filters: role, specialty, location, date, …).
  • Organiser searches for professionals (filters: type, specialty, certifications, languages, availability, verification status, location) and can see each professional's team.
  • Matching / recommendations: filter-based to start; AI-assisted matching, ranking and recommendations in a later phase, alongside saved searches and alerts.

12 — Subscriptions & payments

Subscriptions, payments & verification badge

Professional tiers (monthly)

  • Basic (free) + Bronze + Silver + Gold. What each tier unlocks is defined during Phase 1.

Organisation

  • Free signup; organisations pay for premium services (§13) rather than a subscription.

Payment methods

  • Stripe (cards) first; PayPal added in a later phase.

Verification badge

  • Separate payment; unlocks the verified badge after document review by support.

13 — Payable services

Payable services (private search)

  • The organiser can request a private search — a bespoke shortlist of professionals prepared by the support team.
  • The support team reviews the request and sends a quotation (devis — a price quote).
  • Billed as a one-off service, not a subscription.

14 — Communication

Communication

Messaging

  • In-app messaging between all users: professional ↔ organiser, professional ↔ professional, and with the support team.
  • Direct messaging first; a support chatbot is added in a later phase to handle common questions and triage before a human steps in.

AI features

  • AI is applied especially in search — assisting matching, ranking and recommendations — and powers the later support chatbot.

Notifications

  • In-app + email notifications for applications, invitations, messages, contract / event updates and verification status.

15 — Account verification

Account verification (email & mobile)

  • Email verification at signup.
  • Mobile (SMS) verification.
  • Credential verification is handled separately via document review (§7, §12).

16 — Internationalization

Internationalization (FR / EN)

  • Full user-interface translation in French and English.
  • Bilingual content / data fields where relevant.

17 — Mobile strategy

Mobile strategy

  • Phase 1: mobile-responsive web.
  • Phase 2: optional PWA (installable + push) — an app-like experience.
  • Phase 3: native iOS + Android app via React Native / Expo.

18 — Admin panel

Admin / back-office panel

A private back-office for the internal admin / support team. The early phases ship the essentials needed to run the marketplace; more advanced tooling is added in later phases as paid add-ons.

Included — early phases

  • User & profile management: view and search all professionals and organisations; approve, suspend or ban accounts; edit or correct profile data.
  • Credential verification: review uploaded documents (diplomas, certificates, ordre registration, insurance) and approve or reject them; grant or revoke the verification badge.
  • Subscriptions & payments: see who is on which tier and their payment status; handle verification-badge payments and basic refunds.
  • Payable services & quotations (devis): receive private-search requests; build, send and track quotations (sent, accepted, invoiced, paid).
  • Events, contracts & moderation: view all events (public and sent-to-support); handle direct-to-support events; access contracts; basic moderation of profiles, events and messages.
  • Content & translations: manage static pages (Home, About, Licensing) and FR / EN translations.

Advanced — later phases extra

  • Review & evaluation management: enable or disable evaluations globally or per user; keep reviews private; send private feedback to help a party improve.
  • Analytics dashboards: signups, active users per role, subscription revenue, verification throughput, events posted, application / match rates.
  • Certificate-expiry tracking: automatic flags when a credential lapses.
  • Chatbot & AI management: configure the support chatbot and tune AI-assisted search.
  • Roles, permissions & audit: scoped admin / support sub-roles and a full audit log of admin actions (also useful for GDPR).
  • Advanced moderation & troubleshooting: reports/flags queue, broadcast announcements, and viewing an account “as the user”.

21 — Assumptions & questions

Assumptions, exclusions & open questions

Assumptions

  • Prices are indicative development allocations for an initial build; final quotes depend on confirmed scope, and each phase is delivered and invoiced in sequence.
  • All prices are development only and exclude hosting, domain and every third-party subscription / usage fee (estimated in §20): payment transaction fees, SMS gateway, object storage, email service, AI / LLM usage, maps / other APIs and app-store fees.

Exclusions

  • Native mobile app, reviews, participation history, advanced matching, AI features and the support chatbot — planned for Phase 3.

Open questions to confirm

  • Subscription tiers: confirm the four tiers (Basic free + Bronze / Silver / Gold) and define exactly what each one unlocks.
  • Organisation monetization: free signup only, or also paid tiers in addition to payable services?
  • Verification badge: one-time or recurring payment? Who verifies credentials, and against which authority (ordre des médecins)?
  • Auto-generated contracts: who supplies the base template, and legal review is needed (medical-staffing liability).
  • Teams: can a professional lead several teams and be a member of others at once? Who can remove a member, and what happens to a team application if a member leaves? Can an organiser invite a whole team, or only an individual who then brings the team?
  • Team applications: how is payment / contracting handled for a team engagement — one contract for the team, or one per member?
  • Private search: confirm scope now that team composition is out — is it a shortlist only, and what SLA / pricing model applies?
  • AI features: which model / provider (EU-hosted for GDPR?), what data may be sent to it, and what is the acceptable running-cost ceiling?
  • Support chatbot: scope for the first release — FAQ / triage only, or acting on the account? Escalation path to a human.
  • Organisation profile: finalise the full field list, formats and which fields are mandatory.
  • Reviews & ratings: mutual (both sides) or one-way? Public or private by default?
  • GDPR: sensitive medical & credential data — EU / France hosting, consent management, data export / erasure, document encryption and audit logs.
  • Availability model for professionals (calendar vs. simple ranges).
  • Payment currency and VAT handling.
